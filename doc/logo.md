# creme logo / image-generation prompts

Prompts for generating a logo or illustration for the **creme** programming
language. They lean into the name (cream / dessert), its Scheme/Lisp identity
(lambdas and parentheses), and its self-hosting VM (**icecreme**).

## Primary prompt (logo / mascot)

> A clean, modern logo mascot for a programming language called "creme": a
> glossy swirl of soft cream (like a dollop of whipped crème or gelato)
> sculpted into the shape of a Greek lambda (λ), sitting inside a pair of
> rounded parentheses "( )" that cradle it like a bowl. Warm cream-and-vanilla
> palette with a single accent of raspberry or pistachio. Smooth vector style,
> subtle soft shadows, matte finish, friendly and appetizing but professional.
> Centered composition, plain off-white background, generous negative space.
> Flat modern tech-branding aesthetic, crisp edges, no text.

## Variants

### Minimal flat icon (favicon / app-icon)

> Minimalist flat icon of a cream swirl forming a lambda symbol inside soft
> parentheses, two-tone cream and warm caramel, geometric, rounded corners,
> high contrast, vector, plain background, no text.

### Playful mascot (docs / README hero)

> A cute cartoon mascot: an anthropomorphic scoop of ice cream (nodding to
> "icecreme") holding a tiny code bracket, big friendly eyes, pastel cream and
> mint colors, soft cel-shaded illustration, sitting on a swirl of parentheses,
> cheerful, sticker style, white background.

### Editorial hero banner (landing page)

> Wide banner illustration for a Scheme-based programming language "creme":
> elegant swirls of pouring cream that morph into flowing lambda symbols and
> nested parentheses, silky liquid motion, warm cream/ivory gradient with soft
> golden light, premium and minimal, lots of empty space on the right for a
> headline, 3D soft-render look.

## Notes when generating

- Most models render text badly, so the prompts say "no text" — add the
  wordmark "creme" afterward in a design tool for a clean result.
- For Midjourney, append aspect/quality flags (e.g. `--ar 1:1 --style raw` for
  the logo, `--ar 16:9` for the banner).
